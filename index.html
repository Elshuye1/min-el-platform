OUR STORY

About Min-el

Why we built this

Most small and mid-sized businesses run their operations across a scatter of notebooks, spreadsheets, and messaging apps — sales in one place, stock counts in another, debts remembered rather than recorded. Min-el exists to bring all of that into a single, secure workspace that works the way an owner actually runs a shop: fast at the counter, honest in the books, and connected across every branch and every seller.

Built for Ethiopian business, ready for the world

Min-el started with Ethiopian retailers in mind — multilingual from day one, priced in Birr, and built around a tradition many businesses here already practice: giving back. Every sale recorded through Min-el automatically tracks a 10% charitable tithe, so generosity is part of the ledger, not an afterthought. The same architecture that isolates each company's data also means Min-el scales cleanly to businesses anywhere.

How we think about trust

Every company on Min-el gets its own isolated workspace, enforced at the database level — not just hidden behind a login screen. Sellers only gain access once their Owner approves them. We built the security model first, and every feature since has had to fit inside it.

Where we're headed

Multi-branch reporting, an AI advisor that understands your own sales data, and deeper payment integrations are actively in progress. Min-el is a young, growing platform — feedback from the businesses using it directly shapes what gets built next.

Privacy & Policy

Last updated: . This policy explains what Min-el collects, why, and how it's protected. It is written in plain language and is not a substitute for legal advice.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to anyone using the Min-el platform — Owners, Sellers, and visitors browsing this site before creating an account. "We," "us," and "Min-el" refer to the operator of this platform. "You" refers to the person or business using it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password).
  • Business data: inventory, sales, expenses, debts, wastage records, and anything else you or your team enter into your workspace.
  • Team & company data: business name, branch details, invite codes, and the role/status of each team member.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as browser type and general activity within the platform, used only to keep the service reliable.
  • Billing information: if you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by our payment partner (Chapa); Min-el stores the outcome of a transaction (plan, status, date) but never your card or mobile-money credentials directly.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to operate your workspace, keep your business data synced in real time across your team, send you account-related notices (like a seller approval or a trial expiring), and improve the reliability and security of the platform. We do not sell your business data to third parties, and we do not use your sales or inventory data to advertise to you or anyone else.

4. Company isolation & data separation

Every business on Min-el operates in its own isolated workspace. This isolation is enforced by Firestore security rules at the database level — meaning access control isn't just a matter of what the interface shows you, it's checked on every single read and write. One company's data is never readable by another company's account, by design.

5. Third-party services we rely on

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): authentication, database (Firestore), and file storage.
  • Google Translate: optional, on-demand page translation. If you use it, the visible page text is sent to Google's translation service to be translated; this is unrelated to your account or business data.
  • Chapa: payment processing for paid plans. Chapa handles your card or mobile-money details directly under its own privacy and security practices.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data on our behalf.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and business data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal account information within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain records for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Security

Access to the platform requires authentication through Firebase Authentication. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Company data is scoped and access-controlled through database-level security rules, and every sensitive action (sales, approvals, payments) is recorded with who made it and when.

8. Your rights and choices

You can review and update most of your account information directly from your dashboard. You may request a copy of your data, or request that your account and associated data be deleted, by contacting us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under local data protection law; we honor requests to the extent required by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

Min-el is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

10. Cookies & local storage

We use minimal, functional storage — for example, remembering your selected display language, or keeping you signed in. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Meaningful changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to our support team from within your dashboard, or via the contact details provided on this site.
